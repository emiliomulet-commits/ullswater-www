An insider's guide

How European funding works

EU funding is not a single source but an ecosystem with its own logic, priorities and mechanisms: grants managed directly by the European Commission, funds managed by national and regional authorities, loans from the European Investment Bank and venture capital backed by the European Investment Fund. Understanding how these pieces fit together is the first step towards a successful funding strategy.

The gateway to European venture capital

How EIF capital reaches your company

The EIF, part of the European Investment Bank Group, is Europe's leading developer of risk financing for entrepreneurship and innovation. It does not invest directly in companies: it acts as a cornerstone investor in the European venture capital ecosystem, filling market gaps where high-risk, high-impact innovation struggles to attract purely private capital.

EIF-backed funds bring "smart money" and expertise, and invest in the most promising high-tech companies in sectors such as life sciences, cleantech and ICT. Being investment ready therefore means being prepared to pass the rigorous due diligence of these professional, market-driven investors.

InvestEU

The InvestEU programme is the EU's single investment support mechanism. It gives an EU budget guarantee to implementing partners such as the EIB and the EIF, so they can take on higher-risk projects. It is organised in four policy windows: sustainable infrastructure; research, innovation and digitisation; SMEs; and social investment and skills.
